Seedance 2.5 Prompt:
Reference Lock — @image1:

Use @image1: as the sole identity and wardrobe reference for the adult male skater. Preserve his bald head, medium-brown skin, thin silver oval glasses, dark eyes, full groomed black beard and moustache, muscular athletic build, black short-sleeved T-shirt with its silver geometric chest emblem, dark indigo jeans, and black-and-white skate shoes. His face, beard, glasses, body proportions, clothing, emblem, and shoes remain identical through every cut. His glasses stay securely positioned during every trick. He maintains a calm, self-assured, faintly amused expression.

Scene:

Photoreal live-action skate footage captured on film in a wide Tokyo street at night after rain. Wet pavement reflects dense colored neon from tall buildings on both sides. The street contains a low steel barrier, a long polished granite ledge, and a short flight of steps with a metal handrail. Everything is real, physical, naturally lit, and photographed by real cameras. Fine film grain, visible skin texture, atmospheric depth, and soft highlight roll-off. Real-time movement only; no slow motion.

The skater always travels forward in the direction his body faces. Every trick begins from natural forward momentum and ends with him landing over the board and continuing forward. Maintain believable skateboard physics, foot placement, balance, weight transfer, speed, and momentum.

Skateboard Lock:

One consistent skateboard in every shot: deep-red underside, thin white pinstripe near the nose, cream wheels, silver trucks, and the white katakana マキマ printed once across the center of the underside. Preserve the same deck proportions and markings through all rotations.

0.0–3.0s — FISHEYE FOLLOW:

Begin with a classic low skate-video pursuit shot. A filmer riding a second skateboard tracks beside him at wheel height using an extreme fisheye lens with strong natural barrel distortion. Neon streaks overhead while both boards roll rapidly across the wet pavement. The skater bombs forward, compresses his knees, snaps the tail, and performs one clean ollie over a low steel barrier. The board remains aligned beneath his feet. He clears the barrier, lands firmly on all four wheels, absorbs the impact, and continues forward.

3.0s — HARD CUT.

3.0–5.6s — LOCKED TELEPHOTO KICKFLIP:

A camera is planted still and low on the pavement far down the street. Use an 8° distant telephoto view with no fisheye distortion, shallow depth of field, and strongly compressed neon buildings stacked behind him. He rides directly toward the lens, pops one clean kickflip, the deck rotates exactly once beneath him, and he catches it with both feet. He lands over all four wheels, passes close beside the camera, and exits frame while continuing forward.

5.6s — HARD CUT.

5.6–8.4s — LEDGE-HEIGHT 50-50 GRIND:

The camera sits directly on the polished granite at the near end of the ledge, looking straight along its full length. The skater approaches from the far end, ollies onto the ledge, and locks both trucks into a centered 50-50 grind. He grinds directly toward the lens while growing rapidly in frame. At the near end he pops off cleanly, keeps the board beneath his feet, and passes directly over the camera before landing beyond it.

8.4s — HARD CUT.

8.4–11.0s — LOW HANDRAIL BOARDSLIDE:

Place the camera low on the wet pavement at the bottom of the steps, angled upward along the handrail. At the top, the skater ollies and places the center of the deck perpendicular across the rail. He boardslides smoothly down the entire rail toward the lens while the steps drop away beneath him. He pops free at the bottom, rotates the board back into forward alignment, lands firmly beside the camera, and rolls onward.

11.0s — HARD CUT.

11.0–13.4s — OVERHEAD 360 FLIP:

Cut to a locked high-angle camera looking directly down from a first-floor walkway. The wet street and its neon reflections spread beneath him. He enters below with forward momentum and performs one clean 360 flip. From directly overhead, the deck spins horizontally while flipping, briefly exposing the deep-red underside and centered white マキマ marking. He catches the board beneath his feet, lands cleanly, and continues forward.


13.4s — HARD CUT.

13.4–15.0s — FINAL DEPARTURE:

Return to a locked street-level camera planted low on the wet pavement. The skater rides away down the center of the street, steadily becoming smaller. His broad silhouette remains recognizable between the neon-lit buildings while his reflection stretches across the wet ground beneath him.

Last Frame:

The skater is small but clearly visible in the center background, still riding away. The empty wet street dominates the foreground, carrying long colored reflections toward the camera. Hold this composition through the final moment.

Sound Bed:

Diegetic sound only, synchronized precisely to each action. Wet-city ambience includes distant traffic hissing across wet roads, a passing train, air-conditioning units, neon electrical hum, and water running through a gutter. In the opening shot, two skateboards rumble together until the skater’s tail cracks and four wheels land with a heavy slap. After the first cut, a distant board grows rapidly louder, followed by the kickflip pop, board rotation, landing slap, and fast pass beside the microphone. The 50-50 produces a long stone-and-metal grinding scrape that approaches and passes overhead. The boardslide begins with a hard metallic impact and becomes a bright ringing slide descending toward the microphone, ending with a heavy landing nearby. The overhead trick sounds thin and distant. In the final shot, the wheel rumble steadily recedes into the continuing city ambience. No music.



Capture Realism:

Natural athletic body mechanics and real skateboard momentum. Facial skin remains cinematic matte with visible pores and no plastic smoothing or oily highlights. Preserve physically accurate reflections on his glasses, the skateboard trucks, wet pavement, granite, and steel rail. Clothing responds naturally to speed, landing impacts, and night air. Fine film grain, controlled blacks, soft highlight roll-off, and realistic motion blur.



Text Policy:

No subtitles, captions, logos, overlays, or newly generated readable text. The only intentional lettering is マキマ on the skateboard’s underside. Background neon signage remains environmental and visually indistinct.



Camera Capture:

Wide-latitude live-action film capture with shot-specific fisheye, distant telephoto, ledge-height, rail-height, overhead, and locked street-level cameras; natural 24fps motion blur, real-time playback, hard editorial cuts, fine film grain, 15 seconds.
