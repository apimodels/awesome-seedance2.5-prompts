````text
[INTENT]

A 30-second alternate-history retro scene: a couple at a cliffside table shares a secret handhold in calm, then a nuclear flash births a mushroom cloud matching [Image1] as a shockwave races toward them — serenity curdling into dread.

[LOGIC]

[Image1] is the master reference for final composition, palette, and cloud silhouette: man frame-left in dark suit, woman frame-right in pale dress, both seen from behind at a small round café table with wine bottle and glasses, giant low sun on horizon, barren eroded badlands. Camera never crosses to the front of the couple. Hands-on-laps -> hands joined under table -> flash -> cloud rises to exactly match [Image1] -> shockwave approaches ground-level. Physics: flash precedes cloud; shockwave arrives last. Couple never stands or turns around.

[AESTHETIC]

1950s alternate-history retro-futurism: grainy 35mm film texture, warm sepia-orange palette, sun-bleached rock, deep red sky. Sound palette: dry desert wind, faint distant radio waltz, then total silence with high ringing tone, then sub-bass rumble.

[OPENING]

Locked-off wide shot from behind the couple, table at frame center, man frame-left, woman frame-right, both still with hands resting on their laps; wine bottle and two glasses on the table; vast empty badlands stretching to the horizon under a huge pale sun. [AMBI] soft desert wind, faint tinny big-band waltz from a far-off radio.

[EXECUTION]

[ACT] 0–6s: the couple sits peacefully, barely moving; the woman's head tilts slightly toward the man; their hands remain on their laps. [CAM] static locked-off wide.

[ACT] 6–12s: both hands slip quietly down beneath the table edge and interlace fingers in a gentle squeeze. [SFX] soft fabric shift, faint wooden chair creak, the waltz continues faintly.

[VFX] 12–14s: a blinding white flash blooms on the horizon, swallowing the sun for a beat. [AUDIO] all sound cuts instantly to silence. [EAR] high ringing tone.

[CAM] 14–22s: slow push-in behind the couple. [VFX] a fireball lifts from the horizon, the column climbing and blooming outward into the wide-capped mushroom cloud of [Image1], backlit by the giant sun, orange-red light washing over the land and the backs of the couple. [SFX] low distant roar building.

[ACT] 22–30s: the couple grips hands tighter; the man's shoulders square, the woman leans a fraction closer. [VFX] a dust wall and ground shockwave races across the badlands toward the table. [SFX] deep sub-bass rumble swelling, wind rising to a roar. [CAM] slight handheld tremor as the shockwave nears. [TRANS] final frame shakes hard as the shockwave reaches the table edge — cut out on the impact moment, composition matching [Image1].

Constraints: stable character identity and wardrobe throughout, hands stay joined under the table once joined, cloud silhouette matches [Image1], coherent single light source from horizon, no extra characters, no camera jitter except the final shockwave tremor, no subtitles, no style drift.
````
