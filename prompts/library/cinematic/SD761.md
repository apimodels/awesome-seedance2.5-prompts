## Prompt

````text
FORMAT
5-second cinematic comedy video based directly on the provided reference image. One continuous shot, no cuts.

SUBJECT
A fluffy white cat and a golden retriever are the two main characters. Preserve their exact appearance, fur color, body proportions, relative size, and identity from the reference image throughout the entire video.

ENVIRONMENT
A vast ancient Egyptian desert at golden hour. The enormous Great Sphinx dominates the middle-right background, with a massive pyramid behind it, ancient ruins in the foreground, palm trees, distant camels, and golden sand dunes. Preserve the majestic scale, warm atmosphere, and overall composition of the reference image.

CAMERA
Keep the camera behind and slightly to the side of the animals at the beginning, matching the viewpoint of the reference image. Use a subtle cinematic camera movement that keeps both animals and the Great Sphinx clearly visible. The camera should remain stable and natural throughout the shot. No cuts and no sudden changes of viewpoint.

TIMELINE

00–01s:
The white cat and golden retriever stand quietly on the sandy ridge, looking toward the enormous Great Sphinx. The golden retriever notices the Sphinx and carefully studies its majestic, statue-like posture.

01–02s:
The golden retriever decides to imitate the Sphinx. The dog makes a clear, deliberate 180-degree turn in place, rotating its entire body until it faces directly toward the camera. The complete body rotation is clearly visible and finishes before the dog begins its pose.

02–03s:
Now facing directly toward the camera, the golden retriever sits upright in a dignified Sphinx-like pose. It places its front paws neatly together, raises its chest, holds its head high, and looks extremely serious, carefully imitating the majestic posture of the Great Sphinx behind it.

03–04s:
The golden retriever holds the Sphinx-like pose completely still and seriously, as if proudly believing it has successfully become a miniature Sphinx. The visual comparison between the enormous Great Sphinx in the background and the much smaller dog in the foreground is clearly visible.

04–05s:
The white cat slowly turns its head toward the golden retriever and silently observes the dog's ridiculous but extremely serious Sphinx imitation. The dog continues holding the dignified pose without moving. End while the dog is still maintaining the pose.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Photorealistic cinematic animal animation, realistic natural animal movement, detailed fluffy fur, physically believable sand, warm golden-hour sunlight, subtle atmospheric haze, majestic ancient architecture, strong depth and scale. The humor comes entirely from the serious golden retriever imitating the enormous Great Sphinx. Keep the comedy cute, subtle, natural, and understated rather than cartoonish.

AUDIO
Soft desert wind, subtle movement of sand, quiet animal footsteps, gentle movement sounds as the dog turns and sits, and a very subtle playful musical accent as the dog settles into its Sphinx-like pose.

CONTINUITY AND CONSTRAINTS
The white cat and golden retriever remain the same animals throughout the entire video.
The Great Sphinx remains completely stationary and unchanged.
The pyramid, ruins, palm trees, and distant camel caravan remain consistent with the reference image.
The golden retriever must remain a normal golden retriever throughout the entire video.
The dog imitates the Sphinx only through its posture, orientation, and behavior.
The dog must clearly complete a full 180-degree body rotation before taking the Sphinx-like pose.
The dog must face directly toward the camera while holding the pose.
The dog maintains the Sphinx-like pose from 02s until the end of the video.
Do not make the dog fall, slip, run, jump, bark aggressively, or perform exaggerated cartoon movements.
The white cat remains calm and realistic.
No animal transformation, no morphing, no duplication, no teleportation, no cuts, no sudden camera changes, and no scene changes.
The visual story must remain clearly understandable even if no audio is generated.
````
