## Prompt

````text
Create a realistic 5-second video set in a dungeon containing treasure, based on the provided image.

[Subjects and Objective]
Depict a white cat and a golden retriever rejoicing over the treasure they have found, while a dragon approaches as a threat.

[Timeline]
0–3 seconds: Capture the white cat and golden retriever jumping and frolicking with joy—in a manner natural to their species—in front of the treasure.
3–5 seconds: The camera rapidly zooms in on the dragon in the background, and the dragon's eyes glow ominously.

[Lighting, Camera, and Sound]
Use the light of a sunset streaming into the dungeon and a shallow depth of field that keeps the subjects clearly visible to create a documentary-style realistic look. No dialogue or music.

[Continuity]
The characters consist of one fluffy, long-haired white cat, one golden retriever, and one dragon.
````
