## Prompt

````text
Inspired by: The Teddy Bears' Picnic by Jimmy Kennedy
Audio Track: Continuous sung audio: "If you go down to the woods today, you're sure of a big surprise. / If you go down to the woods today, you'd better go in disguise! / For every bear that ever there was will gather there for certain because / Today's the day the Teddy Bears have their picnic."
Identity & Environment: A sun-dappled old-growth forest clearing centered around a checkered picnic blanket with wicker baskets, teacups, and honey pots. Living vintage teddy bears in worn mohair and velvet secretively gather under leafy daylight. Storybook aesthetic inspired by E.H. Shepard, featuring golden lens flares, pollen motes, warm pastels, and detailed fabric textures.
Camera & Style Direction: Cinematic multi-cut scene with smooth storybook camera movements. Warm nostalgic color palette, tactile fur textures, soft depth-of-field, and dappled canopy light.
0:00 to 0:05 (Shot 1 - Wide Establishing): Low-angle wide tracking shot moving between mossy trunks into a sunlit clearing. Antique teddy bears emerge from thickets carrying baskets to the blanket. End state: Cut as a velvet bear steps into sunlight and reaches a paw forward.
0:05 to 0:10 (Shot 2 - Medium Shot): Medium shot of a stitched mohair bear by an oak root, adjusting a felt cap and woolen scarf as a disguise. It glances around with glass button eyes before waddling off. End state: Cut as the bear waddles out of frame.
0:10 to 0:15 (Shot 3 - Dynamic Medium Shot): Slow tracking shot parallel to a parade of patchwork and golden bears marching side-by-side. One balances a honey pot while another holds a cub's hand. End state: Cut as a bear carrying wild strawberries steps across the lens, wiping the frame.
0:15 to 0:20 (Shot 4 - Tight Close Up / Detail Shot): Tight macro shot of the picnic blanket. Furry paws set down a porcelain cup beside fresh raspberries while golden honey drips onto clover below. End state: Cut as a paw lifts a wooden spoon out of frame.
0:20 to 0:25 (Shot 5 - Over-the-Shoulder Tracking Shot): Over-the-shoulder shot following a tall teddy bear in a tweed vest from behind. Reaching the blanket, it reveals bears clapping paws and sharing treats in glowing light. End state: Cut as the lead bear sits and turns its head toward the camera.
0:25 to 0:30 (Shot 6 - High Angle Extreme Wide Outro): High-angle wide shot pulling back into the canopy. The full picnic is revealed below with dozens of bears across colorful blankets as sunbeams fill the clearing, fading to black.
````
