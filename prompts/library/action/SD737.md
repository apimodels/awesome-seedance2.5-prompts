## Prompt

````text
Seedance 2.5 Prompt
Create a 30-second, 16:9 photorealistic live-action gothic action sequence. Manhattan, a vast luxury penthouse on the highest floor of a skyscraper, immediately before sunrise. The interior looks like deep night behind smoked solar-shield glass. Continuous time, no time-lapse. Cold city light becomes orange sunlight only at the climax.

Action-movie camerawork continues every second throughout the entire video.

Use motivated tracking, low angles, overhead reverses, whip-pans, impact push-ins and exterior wide reveals. Each second has deliberate camera movement; this does not require a cut every second. Preserve screen direction, geography, weapon contact and readable silhouettes. Fast attacks, heavy impacts, brief contact holds; no prolonged slow motion.

REFERENCE LOCK:
Image 1 = the priest, the protagonist BEFORE transformation: the exact adult Black man's face, very short black hair, black clerical robes and bronze cross. Human, upright, controlled.
Image 2 = the SAME protagonist AFTER transformation: the exact enclosed angular helmet, glossy black armor, red chest cross and red details. His weapon is a huge serrated greatsword transformed from his bronze cross, using Image 2's weapon design language. It functions as a sword, never a firearm.
Image 3 = Crimson Lord, the vampire king and boss: the exact bald white head, broad towering silhouette, red horned collar, red cloak, chest cross and enormous curved scythe claws. Taller than the knight.
Image 4 = the vampire soldiers ONLY: pale angular faces, slim hunched bodies, matte black clothing, claws and segmented scythe-tipped tails. REMOVE ALL LONG BRAIDS; keep short slicked-back pale hair. They never resemble the priest.
Keep faces, hair, proportions, costumes and weapons separate. Only the priest transforms into the knight. No duplicate protagonist or duplicate boss. References are appearance sheets, not backgrounds; never show their panels or labels.

SPACE AND CAUSALITY:
Elevator at one end, throne opposite, tall black columns along the sides. The top enclosure includes the glass walls, roof and supporting roof beams. The sunrise horizon is unobstructed once this enclosure is removed. A neighboring building's huge screen is visible at an angle through the smoked glazing. It has a fixed "SUNRISE 6:42" notice and a separate live clock initially reading "6:41". Only the live clock changes to "6:42" near the climax.
The priest's white holy light destroys exposed soldiers but is extinguished by the boss. Other soldiers survive behind columns and later attack. Solar-shield glazing keeps direct sunlight out until the knight removes the upper enclosure. The boss dies from sunlight, not from the sword or the holy light.

SECOND-BY-SECOND ACTION:

00-01: A fast aerial skim approaches the dark Manhattan penthouse. The neighboring screen prominently reads "SUNRISE 6:42", with its separate live clock at "6:41". End the move toward the penthouse windows.

01-02: Low backward tracking from the elevator as its doors slide open. The priest steps out immediately, bronze cross on his chest, robes sweeping behind him.

02-03: Cut to a moving overhead reverse: soldiers cling upside down to the ceiling and sideways to the walls. Their heads snap toward the priest together; scythe tails uncurl around the columns.

03-04: Push from his unchanged human face down to the bronze cross as he raises it. A sharp white holy pulse expands into the first advancing wave.

04-05: Track sideways through the impact: exposed soldiers scream and instantly break into dry ash, blown away from him. Show surviving silhouettes sheltered behind side columns.

05-06: Low dolly toward the far throne. Crimson Lord rises to full height, red cloak dragging over the floor, white face entirely unaffected.

06-07: Tight moving close-up: the boss laughs, deliberately grips his OWN chest cross and holds it up in contempt. The incoming white light visibly collapses into darkness before his hand.

07-08: Whip-pan with the boss's scythe-claw backhand. Clearly show contact with the priest's torso and the same priest thrown sideways across the room, still untransformed.

08-09: Lateral tracking follows the priest into a marble wall. The wall bursts into fragments. He lands in a crouch; his bronze cross returns against his chest and pulses red.

09-10: Tight rising track from boots to torso: articulated glossy black armor expands outward from the red chest seal, enclosing the same body over the robes. Plates lock mechanically, never melt.

10-11: Continue upward as shoulder armor locks and the angular helmet closes around his unchanged head. The red chest cross lights up. His armored hand pulls the original bronze cross free.

11-12: Low three-quarter orbit: the cross's shaft telescopes outward and a huge serrated blade unfolds along it. He stands from the crouch as the completed Image 2 knight, firmly holding the greatsword. Show the complete silhouette.

12-13: Overhead tracking reveals surviving soldiers launching from four directions, including the ceiling. Keep the knight centered and the attack lanes distinct.

13-14: Waist-height lateral tracking follows one powerful sword sweep through the first attackers. At blade contact, their bodies split into streams of dry ash, never flesh or bloody pieces.

14-15: Track just above the floor: the knight ducks a scythe tail that destroys a marble bar behind him, then drives upward with a returning cut. More soldiers dissolve into ash.

15-16: Shoulder-level tracking catches one surviving soldier leaping onto the knight's back and clamping its jaws around his fully armored neck.

16-17: A rapid macro push-in clearly shows the fangs hit the solid black neck guard and SNAP. Tooth fragments fly toward the lens; the armor remains intact.

17-18: Snap back to a readable medium shot as the knight pivots and bisects that attacker with his greatsword. Both halves instantly disintegrate into dry ash. The last soldier is gone.

18-19: Low sideways tracking follows Crimson Lord charging through the drifting ash. His giant scythe claws collide with the knight's raised greatsword, throwing a broad fan of sparks.

19-20: Tight lateral orbit follows a rapid exchange of claw strikes and sword parries. Show actual contacts and recoil; sparks illuminate black armor against the boss's white face and red cloak.

20-21: Floor-level tracking: the boss bears down with crossed scythe claws, overpowering the sword guard. The knight slides backward, boots carving two grooves through the marble floor.

21-22: Push toward the knight's visor as he braces, redirects the claws and frees his blade. His head turns slightly toward the neighboring screen while the boss prepares another charge.

22-23: Fast lens push onto the physical screen outside. The separate live clock switches from "6:41" to "6:42"; "SUNRISE 6:42" remains fixed. The penthouse is still shielded from direct sunlight.

23-24: Low three-quarter tracking: the knight plants his feet and commits to ONE enormous rising diagonal slash. The boss raises his claws to block, but the blade deliberately passes above him. A single razor-thin red cutting plane extends from the sword through the upper glass walls and roof supports, above both characters.

24-25: Match the diagonal into a wide exterior view. Show the COMPLETE upper penthouse enclosure cut through along one continuous inclined seam. Glass, roof and beams separate together as one massive upper section. The floor beneath both fighters stays intact.

25-26: Continue the exterior tracking as the severed upper section visibly slides, tilts and falls away from the tower. Huge glass sheets and structural debris cascade outside. Its removal opens a direct path for blazing orange sunrise into the entire penthouse.

26-27: Cut inside and track the advancing sunlight across the floor onto the boss. His confident white face changes to alarm; dry luminous cracks spread through his face and hands as the sunlight reaches them.

27-28: Pull back to show his full body crumbling from the illuminated side into a collapsing cloud of gray ash. His red cloak loses its supporting body and falls; his OWN chest cross and chain drop with it.

28-29: Low moving close-up follows that same cross striking the marble and landing on the empty red cloak. Ash blows out through the open walls. No intact boss or solid remains.

29-30: A final slow rising arc reveals the knight's powerful silhouette against the orange sunrise, serrated sword lowered, red chest cross still faintly visible. Wind pulls at his cloak amid drifting ash and falling glass outside. End with the knight clearly victorious.

SOUND PRIORITIES:
Distant Manhattan traffic, high-altitude wind, elevator mechanisms, measured footsteps, supernatural holy pulse, soldiers' shrieks, the boss's brief contemptuous laugh, wall impact, locking armor, extending sword, sharp metal clashes, cracking fangs, marble destruction, huge structural shearing, cascading glass and the final metallic cross impact. Wind swells when the enclosure opens. No spoken dialogue or narration. All essential story beats must be understandable with sound off.

RESTRICTIONS:
No blood, gore, exposed organs or bloody severed bodies. Defeated vampires become dry ash immediately. No priest/soldier face or hair mixing. No long braids. No shared weapons or copied tails. No premature sunlight inside. No random explosions replacing the building cut or the boss's dissolution. The boss must visibly become ash, leaving the same red cloak and cross.
No subtitles, speech bubbles, title cards, added logos or watermarks. The only readable text is the requested in-world "SUNRISE 6:42" notice and the live clock. Never omit the transformation, broken fangs, complete upper-section removal or final ash collapse.
````
