## Prompt

````text
Seedance 2.5 Prompt
Create a 30-second, 16:9 photorealistic supernatural action film. A fictional grand museum in New York City, deep at night: a vast Greek sculpture gallery, tall marble columns, glass exhibits, blue moonlight through a skylight, Manhattan lights beyond upper windows. Start inside the gallery with immediate movement. Use readable silhouettes, physical weight, sharp impacts and detailed debris.

Action-movie camerawork continues every second throughout the entire video.
Each one-second beat below has active, motivated camerawork. Beats may connect within continuous shots; do not force a cut every second. Maintain clear geography, show contact before destruction, and preserve the full ending.

REFERENCE ROLES
Image 1: KAIROS, the adult Black male protagonist before transformation. Preserve his face, braided dreadlock bun, sunglasses, black long coat and mechanical limbs.
Image 2: the SAME protagonist after mechanical transformation, wearing the black-and-crimson SKULL LORD armor: horned helmet, skull motifs, red core, layered plates and red hanging fabric. He is the hero, never a separate enemy. Telescoping machinery increases his height from approximately 1.9 m to the reference's 3.3 m. His eyes become completely covered by a sealed opaque visor.
Weapon exception: use a Japanese katana with a clean, mirror-polished blade and black-and-red hilt, replacing the sword pictured in Image 2. Keep this same katana throughout.
Image 3: SERPENT ORACLE, the antagonist. Preserve her adult female appearance, ivory layered robes, extensive cloth coverage, red ancient markings, gold serpent ornaments and black-and-gold snake hair. Her ordinary eyes stay bandaged. Only the forehead binding loosens for the third-eye reveal.

Use the sheets for appearance only. Preserve identities, clothing, materials and colors. Do not reproduce their grids, labels or diagrams. Statues are her petrified human victims, now solid marble throughout. The guards remain intact stone figures at the entrance, outside the main combat area.

STORY RULES
Her snake eyes channel the initial petrification curse while her human eyes remain covered. Her forehead third eye releases the final concentrated curse. Establish one consistent fictional rule: a mirror-polished blade can return that curse to its source.
The hero never exposes his eyes after transformation. His armored optical system reads only her image reflected in the katana during the final maneuver; convey this through the visor-and-blade composition, without HUD graphics.

TIMELINE
00-01: Extreme macro tracking along the woman's wrapped forehead inside the central glass case. Red ancient symbols ignite one after another; a snake shifts beneath a binding. Pull focus to a crack racing across the glass.
01-02: The case bursts outward and all snake heads rise together. A fast backward dolly reveals three approaching guards with raised flashlights; glass sweeps past the lens.
02-03: Whip from a snake's opening eye to the guards. Their faces, hands and uniforms turn gray stone in a fraction of a second. They freeze in their flashlight-aiming poses; the lights remain on.
03-04: Match-cut from a newly petrified guard's terrified face to an ancient marble figure with the same trapped terror. A lateral push reveals its eyes moving and stone fingers grinding against its pedestal.
04-05: A rising diagonal move reveals the gallery's statues stepping down together. Their frightened human expressions and stone-covered personal ornaments reveal the people trapped inside these forms.
05-06: Tilt sharply toward the moonlit skylight. KAIROS crashes downward through it, his recognizable face and braids briefly clear against distant Manhattan lights.
06-07: Floor-level tracking follows his feet into a heavy crouched landing. Boots strike first, knees absorb the impact, marble cracks and glass skitters outward. Hold physical contact clearly.
07-08: A low three-quarter push follows mechanical transformation from the planted boots upward: actuators telescope, limbs lengthen and black-and-red plates unfold and lock. No dissolve or unexplained costume swap.
08-09: Continue rising to the chest core and skull armor. The horned helmet closes around his head; the opaque visor snaps fully shut over both eyes. Red core light reflects on falling glass.
09-10: Track along the katana as he draws it. Its mirror finish briefly reflects the veiled woman behind him, planting the final trick. He immediately slices an approaching marble figure; the visible impact breaks it into stone.
10-11: Lateral tracking: he ducks a second statue's punch and cuts upward through its solid marble torso. Angular fragments scatter across the floor, with dry dust and no organic material.
11-12: A low forward drive follows his shoulder-check into a third statue. It flies into a column, fractures on impact and knocks a large section from the column.
12-13: Follow the falling column section as he vaults over it. He lands into a downward strike that shatters a fourth statue and chips the floor beneath it.
13-14: Side-on tracking keeps the cause and result in frame: he kicks a broken pedestal into two advancing statues, driving them through glass display cases.
14-15: Sweep around his sword side as he pivots through a waist-height slash. Three tightly grouped marble attackers break apart in sequence along the blade's path.
15-16: A close rear tracking shot catches another statue grabbing his cloak. He breaks its stone hand with an elbow, turns and drives the sword hilt into its chest, cracking it apart.
16-17: Track the last charging statue across the central aisle. His diagonal cut sends its two stone sections into an already damaged column, collapsing the column into the empty side gallery.
17-18: Pull back through settling rubble to reveal the woman beginning to float above her broken case. The hero pivots toward her; the ruined hall remains visible between them.
18-19: Crane upward beside the floating woman. Her layered robes billow as eight principal hair-snakes extend 20-30 meters across the vast hall, retaining the reference's patterned scales.
19-20: Track one enormous snake striking toward the hero. He dashes sideways; the head smashes the exact floor position he occupied, spraying marble chips.
20-21: A ground-skimming lateral move follows him sliding under two crossing snakes. Their bodies strike opposing columns and tear through more display cases behind him.
21-22: Pull wide as snakes coil around tall columns and wrench them apart. The hero springs over a falling beam while another snake lunges through the opening beneath his boots.
22-23: Track his controlled landing onto a clear strip of floor. He deliberately turns his back toward the woman as her snakes recoil for a final attack. The camera continues moving over his shoulder.
23-24: Push toward her forehead. Only its bandage unwinds; her ordinary eyes remain covered. A closed third eye begins to open beneath the glowing red symbol.
24-25: Track beside the hero's sealed visor. With his back still toward her, he raises the polished katana near his face, slightly offset beside his head. Her veiled face and opening third eye are clearly reflected in the blade.
25-26: Move to a readable side angle showing woman, blade and hero. The third eye opens and sends a narrow supernatural light past his shoulder onto the angled blade; the blade visibly reflects it back into her third eye.
26-27: Push toward her as gray stone rapidly spreads from her forehead across her face, robes and snake hair. Show her entire form become solid marble before the strike. The extended snakes stiffen and stop.
27-28: A fast waist-height arc follows the hero turning through one precise horizontal slash. The blade cuts across her already petrified torso, leaving a clean fracture. His visor remains completely closed.
28-29: Glide past her stone face as hairline cracks spread through the figure and its petrified snakes. She drops gently onto the broken plinth and begins crumbling into dry marble fragments.
29-30: A low forward glide follows her gold serpent medallion falling onto the floor. Her entire stone figure finishes collapsing into an inert rubble mound in the background. One delicate metal clink ends the scene; the armored hero stands beyond the settling dust.

AUDIO
BGM enabled: an original tense cinematic score. Sparse low strings and pulsing bass during the awakening; driving percussion enters with the landing and transformation, accelerating through the battle. Drop the percussion when he raises the reflective blade. Cut the score at the reflected curse's impact and keep it absent through the quiet final collapse.
Use spacious museum ambience, distant NYC traffic, glass bursts, servo locks, heavy footsteps, metallic sword sounds, grinding marble, columns breaking, snake hisses, falling stone and the final medallion clink. Keep decisive effects audible over the music. No dialogue, narration or vocals.

CONSTRAINTS
No subtitles, captions, speech bubbles, title cards, logos or watermarks. Keep the requested ancient symbols as physical markings on the bandages and robes. No blood, gore or exposed tissue; destroyed figures are solid stone. Preserve the woman's layered clothing. No extra protagonist, identity swapping, exposed hero eyes, teleporting transformation, constant slow motion, camera roll, obscuring impact flashes or explosion at the ending. Make the petrification, mechanical transformation, reflection and final collapse understandable visually even without sound.
````
