Seedance 2.5 Prompt
30 seconds, 16:9. A LIVE-ACTION theatrical samurai action film, photographed with real human actors, a real black horse, physical costumes, full-size timber streets and real canal locations. Bright sunny midday in Guren no Suito. Preserve the complete rescue, transformation, duel and ending below.

LIVE-ACTION APPEARANCE — HIGHEST PRIORITY
Every frame must look like photographed live-action footage. Natural facial proportions, visible skin pores, individual hairs, irregular skin tone, real eyes and natural expressions. Armor has physical thickness, overlapping plates, tiny surface variations and restrained metallic reflections. Cloth has visible weave, weight and inertia. The horse has natural anatomy, hair and muscular motion.
Use direct sunlight, believable reflected light, contact shadows, natural exposure, restrained saturation and subtle film grain. Keep detail in skin, pale masks and dark armor. Fire illuminates nearby surfaces; dust, smoke and water have photographic texture. Supernatural action uses seamless feature-film VFX composited into live-action photography. Maintain this photographic appearance during transformation, aerial combat and explosions.
No anime rendering, cartoon animation, cel shading, illustrated textures, stylized 3D characters, game cinematics, plastic skin, doll faces, toy armor, painted highlights or artificial bloom. Do not reproduce the reference sheets' presentation style.

Action movie camera work continues every 1 second.
Use the camera positions and shot sizes below, with decisive changes in height, distance, perspective and foreground depth. One-second entries describe action and camera development, not compulsory cuts. Keep the rescue and transformation legible within coherent moving shots. Alternate detail shots, wide environmental shots, overhead views and low angles. Do not repeatedly frame both fighters waist-up from the side.
Camera movement must have physical weight, natural motion blur and strong foreground parallax. Brief impact vibration only. No perpetual orbit, floating virtual camera or random spinning. Show contact before destruction. Superhuman speed comes from explosive acceleration, rapid parries and short photographic motion trails, not drawn speed lines or glowing duplicate bodies.

REFERENCE IDENTITIES
Image 1 = KOUSEI NO GYOUSHA, the hero before transformation: the same dark-skinned face, muscular body, red hooded robe, cream star motifs, waist rope, waist oni mask and katana.
Image 2 = KOGA TAISHO, the same hero transformed: exact red-and-black armor, curved horns, red oni faceplate, gold fittings, cloak and katana. Body about 2.3 m tall, about 2.6 m including horns.
Image 3 = ONI-TOURYOU, the enemy: exact white oni mask with hinged lower jaw, pale antler-like horns, white fur mantle, black floral robes, armor and katana. Body about 1.9 m tall.
Image 4 = SAKURAI IROHA: same face, black braids, red hair ribbons, patterned kimono and obi. Rescued unharmed.
Image 5 = GUREN NO SUITO: castle, merchant houses, tiled roofs, canals, vermilion bridges, lanterns and noren curtains. Use clear midday sunlight, not dusk.
Preserve faces, costume designs and silhouettes while rendering them as photographed people and physical objects. No design simplification. No swapped masks, weapons or identities. The hero's two forms are one person, never two separate fighters. Do not show reference labels, borders or diagrams.

FIRE AND CONTINUITY
ONLY Oni-Touryou breathes fire, directly from the opening in his WHITE mask. Koga never breathes fire, emits fire from his body or grows flame wings. His katana separates an incoming enemy fire stream; it does not generate that stream. Show the source and direction clearly.
The merchant street borders a canal. Kousei rides down a vermilion arched bridge. Iroha and the horse are left with evacuees behind a thick stone embankment before the duel. Keep them there, away from debris. Later combat destroys evacuated shops and a DIFFERENT empty bridge. Preserve damage across shots.
