````text
Ultra-realistic cinematic 8K futuristic space-opera duel on a rain-soaked landing platform at night, 12-second continuous shot. Two futuristic warriors stand several meters apart on a vast metallic platform overlooking a distant alien city. Heavy rain falls continuously, bouncing off the metal floor and forming shallow reflective puddles. Neon lights, distant spacecraft and industrial structures glow through the mist and rain. Both warriors wear distinctive advanced combat clothing from a distant future, combining practical armored elements, worn fabrics, technological details and subtle illuminated components. Their designs feel like elite warriors from an ancient galactic civilization adapted to a technologically advanced future. Neither resembles a modern soldier or astronaut. Their equipment feels functional, believable and technologically advanced. Each warrior holds a futuristic energy weapon with an unlit blade. The weapons resemble elegant plasma or energy blades, but have an original futuristic design rather than any recognizable existing weapon. Seconds 0–3: The camera begins at a low angle between the two warriors, slowly moving sideways as rain falls heavily around them. Both warriors remain completely still, facing each other across the wet platform. Their clothing and coats move subtly in the wind. The atmosphere is tense and silent except for heavy rain and distant machinery. Seconds 3–5: The camera begins a slow, smooth orbit around both warriors, gradually revealing their silhouettes against the distant alien city. Neither warrior moves toward the other. They maintain eye contact and remain ready for the confrontation. Seconds 5–6: Almost simultaneously, both warriors activate their energy weapons. Two brilliant energy blades ignite with a sharp burst of light, illuminating their faces, clothing and the rain around them. Reflections of the glowing blades appear across the wet metal floor. Seconds 6–8: The camera continues its controlled cinematic orbit around the two warriors, keeping both clearly visible in the frame. They slowly shift into guarded fighting stances. Rain streams from their clothing while the glowing blades cast colored light through the mist. Seconds 8–9: The first warrior suddenly takes one deliberate step forward and performs a controlled diagonal strike toward the second warrior. The second warrior immediately raises their energy blade and blocks the attack. The two blades collide with a brief shower of bright sparks. Seconds 9–10: The second warrior pushes the first blade aside and performs one controlled horizontal counterattack. The first warrior steps back and blocks the counterattack cleanly. Seconds 10–11: The first warrior immediately responds with a second deliberate strike from the opposite direction. The second warrior parries the attack and both warriors briefly lock blades before separating. Seconds 11–12: Both warriors take a small step backward and return to guarded fighting stances, facing each other across the rain-soaked platform. Their glowing blades remain raised as the camera completes its smooth orbit around them. End with both warriors still facing each other, ready for the next exchange. The sword fighting must remain controlled, deliberate and physically believable. Each attack and block is clearly separated and easy to follow. No frantic choreography, no rapid spinning, no acrobatics, no excessive movement and no chaotic combat. The two warriors maintain consistent positions relative to each other throughout the sequence. One continuous uninterrupted cinematic shot. No cuts. No scene changes. No teleportation. No additional characters. Keep both warriors visually consistent throughout the entire sequence. Keep the camera movement smooth and controlled, with both warriors remaining clearly visible during the combat exchange. Dramatic cinematic composition, photorealistic rain, realistic reflections, detailed futuristic clothing and equipment, atmospheric fog, volumetric lighting, subtle environmental movement, believable futuristic technology, strong depth of field, epic space-opera atmosphere, dark alien world, smooth controlled camera movement, cinematic 8K realism, single continuous shot, restrained choreography. No modern military equipment. No contemporary astronaut suits. No recognizable existing characters or weapons.
````
