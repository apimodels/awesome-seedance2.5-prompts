This is a friendly but intense training fight between two superhuman warriors who know each other very well. The tone is explosive physical combat contrasted with casual, familiar conversation between old friends.
Scene:
In a stormy rocky training ground filled with shallow water and jagged ruined stone, Tatyana and Raine are trading powerful blows at close range. Rain falls hard, water splashes with every movement, and their punches create bursts of energy on impact. The camera stays close and cinematic, capturing the force of their strikes, the spray of water, and their facial expressions. Even while exchanging brutal hits, they are relaxed and teasing, talking like old friends catching up.

Tatyana and Raine trade rapid punches and blocks in the water, both focused but clearly enjoying themselves. Tatyana asks, “How is Saffron?”

Tatyana laughs through the hit, recovers instantly, then counters hard, shattering Raine’s diamond barrier and landing a clean strike. Tatyana says, “Surprised someone could tie me down?”

Tatyana should laugh naturally during the exchange. Raine should look confident, dryly amused, and sharp. Their banter should feel effortless and lived-in.

Keep both women recognizable from the source image. Maintain the same red-haired Tatyana and dark-haired Raine. Preserve the soaked clothing, muscular athletic physiques, and stormy ruined setting.
