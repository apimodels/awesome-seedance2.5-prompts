## Prompt

````text
The orange ball rolls naturally along the metal floor and clearly comes into direct contact with a small mechanical control device embedded in the floor.

The moment the ball touches the device, the device visibly activates: a bright blue indicator suddenly illuminates and several small mechanical components begin moving.

The activation is clearly caused by the ball's contact with the device and should be visually understandable even without audio.

Immediately afterward, visible mechanical reactions spread through the surrounding facility. Nearby pipes vibrate subtly, pressure gauges move, valves rotate, gears begin turning, and dormant machinery starts operating.

Through the enormous windows, several distant lights inside the underwater city suddenly illuminate one after another, revealing that the activation has affected a much larger system.

The visual progression should be unmistakable:
the ball hits the device → the device activates → nearby machinery reacts → the distant underwater facility comes alive.

Use the small blue-lit mechanical device already visible on the floor in the reference image as the activation device.
````
