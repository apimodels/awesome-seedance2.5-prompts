Seedance 2.5 Prompt
30-second video, 16:9. Photoreal live-action big-budget action film, bright midday sunlight, realistic human skin, soaked wet fabric, heavy armor, convincing creature effects and massive water destruction.

Action movie camera work continues every 1 second.

No introduction. The SEA BEAST is clearly visible from FRAME 0. Every second contains purposeful cinematic camerawork; continuous shots may bridge intervals. Fast combat, readable impacts and geography, with brief contact-only slow motion.

REFERENCE LOCK
Image 1: THE DIRECTOR, Emma's father, 188 cm. Preserve his exact face, skin tone, short hair, beard, sunglasses, navy double-breasted suit, gold-patterned tie, gold accessories and leather shoes.
Image 2: CROSS DEVIL, the transformed DIRECTOR, 3.2 m. Preserve the exact horned black helmet, red eyes, white-and-black armor, red chest cross, glowing red seams, red cape, segmented armored tail and huge black-and-red Crossbringer sword. These are two forms of ONE person.
Image 3: ONE SEA BEAST, 12 m tall when upright in this video. Override the reference sheet's larger size annotations. Match its exterior head, pale mottled blue-gray skin, dark raised spots, red eyes, facial tentacles, sucker-lined mouth and octopus limbs. No human legs. Completely ignore anatomical cutaways, organs, exposed cross-sections and diagram panels.
Image 4: EMMA CARTER, the same child, face, brown curls, yellow sweater with dark stripes, jeans, yellow backpack and yellow high-top shoes. She holds an octopus plush throughout, tucked in one arm during the rescue. She is never injured.
Preserve identities, proportions, costumes and weapons across all shots. Only CROSS DEVIL has the chest cross, armored tail and Crossbringer.

LOCATION
Stay inside ONE huge American aquarium atrium, including underwater inside its main tank. A 15 m acrylic tank dominates the rear wall; smaller tanks flank it. Strong daylight enters through the intact skylight. Families initially fill the hall. Raised exit stairs with a mid-height landing remain at frame right.
All attacking arms belong to the SAME SEA BEAST, reaching around display partitions and smashing outward through the small tanks. No extra monsters. Keep people, stairs and tank architecture in wide shots to communicate scale.

CONTINUITY STATE (damage and wetness only accumulate; nothing is ever restored)
MAIN TANK: Intact only in 00-02. From 02s onward, the UPPER HALF of the front acrylic panel is GONE: one huge jagged hole with broken edges and hanging shards. Water pours continuously over the JAGGED BREAK EDGE, never over the top rim. The LOWER front panel survives, covered in spiderweb cracks, holding a lowered waterline at about half the tank height. Every later shot that shows the main tank must show this hole, the cracks and the lowered waterline. The main tank never again appears whole, full or uncracked.
SMALL TANKS: From 03s, the flanking small tanks are smashed open, empty, with shattered fronts and spilled gravel and coral. They never appear intact again.
FLOOR: From 03s, the whole atrium floor is flooded ankle-deep, littered with acrylic fragments and floating debris, reflecting the skylight. Every step, slide and impact throws spray. The floor is never dry again.
PEOPLE: From the 02-03 water wall onward, THE DIRECTOR and EMMA are completely drenched. His hair and beard are wet and dripping, his navy suit is darkened, heavy matte wool clinging to his body, water running from his sleeves and trouser hems, droplets on his sunglasses. Emma's curls are wet and heavy, her sweater, jeans, backpack and plush are darkened and dripping. They stay visibly soaked in every later shot, including the final two-shot. The wet suit is matte wool, never shiny leather.
CROSS DEVIL: His armor and cape are streaked with water and dark ink droplets from his first slash onward.
EMMA POSITION: From 08s until 28s, Emma stays on the stair landing at frame right, behind the railing, above the flood. Whenever she appears during the battle, she is on that landing, never on the ground floor and never near a tentacle.

SECOND-BY-SECOND ACTION

00-01:
Frame 0: extreme-wide floor-level push-in. The SEA BEAST's huge face and suction-covered arms already press against the intact acrylic, red eyes glowing. Tiny Emma holds her plush in the foreground beside THE DIRECTOR. Show the enormous face and small girl together.

01-02:
Over Emma's shoulder, crash-zoom into the red eyes. The first tentacle strike hits the upper pane. Snap to a traveling macro of spiderweb cracks racing across the acrylic from the impact.

02-03:
The second strike blasts the UPPER HALF of the front panel outward, leaving the jagged hole; the cracked lower panel holds. Hold fragments and droplets in extreme slow motion for 0.25 seconds, then ramp to real time. Frontal ultra-wide: the water wall engulfs the lens as THE DIRECTOR shields Emma. Both are completely drenched, but neither is struck by debris. The surge pushes them a few meters back together.

03-04:
Handheld tracking weaves between fleeing families splashing through the newly flooded floor. Long arms smash outward through the small tanks in a chain of water bursts, leaving them shattered and empty. Their visible paths lead back to the main tank, where water still pours over the jagged break edge. THE DIRECTOR, soaked, rises to one knee a step from Emma.

04-05:
Whip-pan between attacking tentacles, then drop to floor level as one coils around Emma's shoe-covered ankle and pulls her across the flooded floor, throwing up a wake of spray. Her wet curls whip; she keeps the dripping plush. No injury or collision.

05-06:
Floor-skimming tracking at water level follows Emma's short slide. THE DIRECTOR slides in on his knees through the shallow flood, spray fanning out, and catches her wrist before she can be pulled farther.

06-07:
Tight moving close-up of their joined wet hands, water dripping from his soaked cuff. Widen along their arms as he braces a foot against a fixed display base, stops her movement and pulls her toward him.

07-08:
Low side tracking: both dripping. He tears the tentacle away with his free hand, gets Emma onto her feet and firmly guides her up the exit stairs to the landing. She stays there, behind the railing and above the flood, holding the wet toy. The smashed small tanks are visible behind them.

08-09:

09-10:

Cut low; dolly-zoom as he grows into the full 3.2 m CROSS DEVIL, standing in the flood. Crossbringer deploys into his right hand. Emma on the stair landing and the stair rail remain visible as scale references, with the broken main tank behind. His first step splashes directly into combat.

12-13:

14-15:
Extreme low angle at water level between armored feet. He cuts upward through a descending fourth tentacle, blade passing near the lens. Brief 0.3-second impact slowdown, then fast recovery.

15-16:
Whip to his rear quarter. His armored tail coils around an attacking arm behind him and slams it into the flood, throwing out a circular sheet of water. The sword stays in his right hand. Emma is visible on the distant stair landing.

16-17:
Rising three-quarter track: he plants the sword TIP in the flooded floor as a brief pivot, rotates his body, visibly pulls the blade FREE, then completes a broad circular slash through surrounding arms. Show pivot, release and cutting arc in order.

17-18:
Extreme low angle: the SEA BEAST heaves its upper body up and out THROUGH the jagged hole in the upper front panel, reaching 12 m upright. Broken acrylic edges and hanging shards frame its body; water spills over the break edge around it, and the cracked lower panel with its lowered waterline is clearly visible. Facial tentacles part to reveal the sucker-lined mouth. This creature hero shot lasts under two seconds; no anatomical imagery.

18-19:
A thick arm wraps CROSS DEVIL's torso and yanks him upward through the jagged opening toward the water retained in the lower half of the tank. Keep his sword arm free. The camera rises rapidly alongside the pull, passing the broken edge.

19-20:
Follow him through the lowered waterline inside the tank: a brief split-level shot shows the jagged broken panel and bright atrium above, blue water below, then the camera plunges underwater beside him. Preserve tank geography and motion direction.

20-21:
Underwater side tracking: the SEA BEAST jets dense black ink between itself and CROSS DEVIL. The spreading cloud swallows the blue daylight shafts and reaches the lens.

21-22:
Almost total black ink, with only red eye points visible. Maintain a subtle sideways camera drift. This is moving water, not a fade or transition.

22-23:
Push toward CROSS DEVIL as his chest cross illuminates the ink FROM WITHIN. Crimson volumetric light reveals his armored silhouette and bubbles. The cross belongs to him alone.

23-24:
Slide into a profile two-shot: both pairs of red eyes face each other across a short gap, bodies revealed by chest light and returning blue shafts. Bubbles appear suspended for a fraction of a second; sound drops nearly silent. The camera keeps creeping.

24-25:
Track along Crossbringer as its red edge intensifies. CROSS DEVIL sets his grip against the restraining arm and prepares ONE decisive upward slash.

25-26:
Ultra-wide side view, still underwater: one explosive upward cut severs the binding arms, releasing only ink and water. The SEA BEAST recoils and its arms go slack. The force of the slash strikes the cracked LOWER front panel from inside. Simultaneously pull the camera backward up through the waterline and out through the jagged upper hole into the atrium.

26-27:
Exterior view OF THE DAMAGED TANK, still inside the hall: its upper front is already missing, edged with jagged acrylic. Now the cracked LOWER panel splits vertically from the floor up to the existing hole. Its sections separate and the retained water erupts outward across the flooded hall. The defeated SEA BEAST falls back behind ink and spray; no exposed wound. The main surge flows away from Emma's stair landing.

27-28:
Lateral tracking catches skylight refracting through spray into a natural rainbow. Brief slow motion, then return to real time. Spray crossing the lens creates a motivated wipe into the human return.

28-29:
After the mist wipe, THE DIRECTOR is human again: exact Image 1 appearance, still completely soaked, hair and beard dripping, his navy suit dark, heavy and clinging, sunglasses back on with water droplets. Track backward as he wades through ankle-deep water and floating debris toward the stairs. Emma, wet curls and damp sweater, comes down to the bottom step; he extends his hand.

Behind them, the ruined hall is clearly visible: the split, empty main tank, shattered small tanks and the flooded floor. Emma smiles and raises the SAME soaked octopus plush, water dripping from its arms. Use room ambience, water pressure, heavy tentacle impacts, acrylic cracking, huge splashes, fleeing footsteps sloshing through water, distant nonverbal crowd reactions, wet sliding, armor mechanisms, cape movement, blade whooshes, tail impact and underwater bubbles. Maintain cinematic camerawork every second through the final medium shot.
