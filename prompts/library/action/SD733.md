Seedance 2.5 Prompt
Target duration: 30 seconds. Aspect ratio: 16:9.
Photoreal live-action pirate fantasy, cinematic practical textures, fast physical action, dark humor. Bright midday, cloudless blue sky, vivid blue open ocean. One isolated, dense wall of fog surrounds only the ghost ship's arrival; the surrounding sea stays sunlit. Start with the collision already happening, with no introductory shot.

REFERENCE ASSIGNMENTS AND CONTINUITY
@Image 1 = the single human pirate captain. Preserve his reference face, eyepatch placement, beard, decorated dreadlocks, weathered brown tricorn, distressed long coat, reddish sash, belts, boots, curved cutlass and two distinct flintlock pistols. Never mirror his eyepatch.
@Image 2 = the single leader of the ghost ship: the approximately 4.2-meter-tall, muscular gray sea demon. Preserve his curled horns, glowing red eyes, red markings, dark ornate armor, skull ornaments and thick octopus tentacles with suction cups. His tentacles are attached to his own body. He fights with tentacles in this scene.
@Image 3 = the enemy skeleton pirate CREWMEN, appearing as multiple members of the same crew. Preserve their exposed weathered bones, red bandanas, ragged dark pirate clothes, belts, boots and cutlasses. They are ordinary crew, not additional captains or copies of the sea demon.
The captain's allies are living adult human pirates with varied faces and outfits. They become wildly amused and exhilarated during the fight. Keep the two crews visually distinct. Use the reference sheets only for character appearance; never show sheet layouts, labels or reference backgrounds.

GEOGRAPHY, PROPS AND CAMERA
The black ghost ship strikes the pirate ship at an oblique angle, then grinds alongside its starboard side. Both decks remain close enough for boarding and the final confrontation. The captain stays above or on his own deck; the demon attacks from the adjacent ghost ship's bow. Show their positions clearly before close-ups.
The captain uses pistol A once against a skeleton and pistol B once for the finale. Both are single-shot flintlocks; no repeated firing without reloading. The final attack uses his last personally carried rum bottle. A crewmate gives him a different drinking bottle afterward.
Action-movie camerawork continues every second throughout the entire video.
The one-second intervals are action beats, not a requirement for thirty separate cuts. Link compatible beats with continuous tracking. Alternate readable wide shots, lateral tracking, low angles, controlled whip pans and close-ups. Preserve screen direction. Show attacks connecting before showing their consequences. Keep most action at full speed; use only a tiny speed ramp for the finishing slash. Camera shake is brief and tied to impacts.

SECOND-BY-SECOND ACTION
00–01s: Waterline wide tracking shot. A black ghost-ship prow bursts through the localized fog wall and immediately crashes into the pirate ship's side. A huge spray erupts; railing fragments and timber splinters fly toward the lens. Blue sea and clear sky remain visible.

01–02s: Knee-height tracking across the jolting human deck. Pirates stagger and grab ropes as both ships' cannons fire, recoiling through rolling powder smoke. Fresh wood fragments scatter across the planks; the hulls grind together with crushing weight.

02–03s: A rapid push from the ghost ship's bow into @Image 2's face reveals the demon before the third second. His red eyes flare. In the foreground, a thick gray tentacle clamps around the bow rail, suction cups compressing against wood.

03–04s: Wide view across the gap. @Image 3 skeleton pirates leap and swing from the ghost ship onto the human deck. The camera tracks their movement from the black hull toward the living pirates.

04–05s: Low dolly backward as the first skeletons land hard on the planks and charge with cutlasses. More skeletal crew arrive behind them. Human pirates recover their balance and meet the boarding party.

05–06s: Tight lateral tracking beside a grinning human pirate. His cutlass strikes a skeleton's skull; dry bone fractures visibly, then the whole skeleton collapses into clattering pieces. Show contact and collapse in the same readable movement.

06–07s: A short camera arc follows another human pirate laughing uproariously as he knocks a skeleton's weapon aside and smashes its skull. The enemy falls apart at his boots while the surrounding melee continues.

07–08s: Waist-height tracking reveals @Image 1 driving through the fight. A powerful right-handed cutlass sweep breaks through two charging skeletons, scattering loose bones across the deck. His coat and sash whip with his turn.

08–09s: Three-quarter medium shot. The captain fires pistol A from his left hand into an approaching skeleton. A brief muzzle flash and powder cloud accompany its skull breaking and body collapsing. His right hand keeps the cutlass ready.

09–10s: Continue tracking beside him. He returns the spent pistol A to his belt while cutting down the next attacker with his right hand. Loose bones skid away. He turns his attention toward the adjacent ghost ship.

10–11s: Low-angle push toward the captain. He raises his cutlass above the battle and shouts exactly one clear English command: "Rum!" His expression is fierce and delighted. The nearby human pirates respond with reckless laughter.

11–12s: A fast lateral move passes a row of laughing human pirates holding cloth-topped rum bottles. The cloth tips catch fire, illuminating their excited faces. Keep this a brief cinematic action beat.

12–13s: Rise into a wide tracking view as the pirates throw their burning bottles together. The fiery arcs cross the gap toward the ghost ship; follow the leading bottle without losing the location of either vessel.

13–14s: The camera follows the leading bottle into the ghost ship's rigging. Glass shatters against wood and canvas. A stylized fantasy flash fire races along the tattered sails and mast, turning the black ship into a towering orange blaze.

14–15s: High oblique wide shot of the ghost deck. Skeleton pirates are engulfed in flames and collapse into heaps of scorched bones. Burning sailcloth falls behind them. The blue sky and sea make the fire sharply visible.

15–16s: Descend toward the burning bow. @Image 2 crawls out through the flames, hauling his massive body over broken timbers. His entire silhouette burns, but the gray face, curled horns, armor and tentacles remain recognizable.

16–17s: Low-angle push as the burning sea demon rises with a furious roar. His red eyes lock on the human crew. Several attached tentacles coil back and then lash toward their deck with immense weight.

17–18s: Fast sideways tracking follows a tentacle sweeping living pirates off their feet. They tumble and skid across their own deck, knocking over barrels. Splinters explode from a struck railing; no human dismemberment or blood.

18–19s: Whip pan to the captain as one tentacle coils around his torso below his shoulders. It yanks him off the deck, leaving both arms free. His boots lift toward the demon's face level.

19–20s: Crane upward with the suspended captain, still above the inside edge of his own deck. He sheathes the cutlass with his right hand and pulls his last personal rum bottle from his sash with his left. He gives the raging demon a defiant grin.

20–21s: Tight side view containing the captain, bottle and demon's open mouth. Pulled close to the monster's face, the captain drives the bottle firmly between its jaws. The bottle remains visibly wedged there as he withdraws his left hand.

21–22s: Over the captain's right shoulder, he draws the still-loaded pistol B with his right hand and fires once through the visible bottle. Show the shot breaking the glass, followed immediately by a cinematic orange fireburst engulfing the demon's head. No exposed tissue.

22–23s: Track downward as the demon recoils and the gripping tentacle uncoils. The captain drops free toward his own deck. He releases the spent pistol B and reaches for his cutlass with his right hand.

23–24s: Readable side tracking during the fall. The captain draws the cutlass and slices cleanly through the withdrawing tentacle. A very brief speed ramp emphasizes blade contact; the severed end visibly separates through sparks and smoke, with no blood or wound close-up.

24–25s: Deck-level tracking catches his boots and one knee striking the planks. He slides a short distance, cutlass extended, then rises. The severed tentacle segment lands separately behind him; loose bones rattle from the impact.

25–26s: Crane back to show both ships. The burning ghost ship tears free of the side contact and lists away. The wounded sea demon staggers backward on its collapsing bow as its remaining tentacles withdraw from the human vessel.

26–27s: Wide view from the human deck. The demon and the ghost ship's bow plunge into the blue sea together. Water surges upward, steam billows, and the remaining burning masts tilt as the ship continues to sink.

27–28s: Track through the surviving human pirates raising rum bottles, clinking them together and laughing in triumph. One crewmate hands the captain a fresh, intact, unlit drinking bottle from their supplies. Burning wreckage sinks in the background.

28–29s: Medium close-up. The captain takes a confident swig from the fresh bottle, with the sinking ghost ship clearly behind him. His crew continues its wild celebration around him.

29–30s: A gentle final push into the captain's face. He lowers the bottle and gives a crooked, satisfied grin beneath his tricorn. End on the grin, lively laughter and a final bottle clink, with the burning wreck settling behind him.
