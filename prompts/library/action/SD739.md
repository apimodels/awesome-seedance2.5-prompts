Seedance 2.5 Prompt
SEEDANCE 2.5 — BLADE SHERIFF vs. POLICE GREMLIN — THE GOLD VAULT

Duration: 30 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 16:9.
Style: photorealistic live-action action-thriller with a dry comic payoff. Realistic skin, leather, fur, gold, weight, and cinematic motion blur. Bright, luxurious golden lighting throughout.

Action movie camera work continues every 1 second.

Give every one-second interval a motivated cinematic camera movement. Adjacent intervals may continue the same shot; preserve geography and screen direction.

REFERENCE LOCK

Reference image 1 = BLADE SHERIFF only:
One 188 cm adult man, the exact reference face, swept-back brown hair, beard, black sunglasses, long dark leather trench coat, black shirt, tactical trousers, harness, and black leather engineer boots. One reference-matching revolver in his right-side holster. Maintain his identity and proportions throughout.

Reference image 2 = POLICE GREMLIN only:
Approximately 80 cm tall, enormous triangular ears, purple and pink fur, red eyes, uneven fangs, clawed hands and feet, a long furry tail, a blue police cap with red trim and gold badge, and the reference uniform shoulder patches/armbands. Preserve the reference anatomy and costume.

There are exactly SIX gremlins: one original sentry plus five identical-looking reinforcements. All six use reference image 2, remain approximately 80 cm tall, and retain separate bodies and consistent positions. No additional creatures.

Use both character sheets only for appearance. Do not show their layout, labels, diagrams, or backgrounds.

LOCATION AND CONTINUITY

One enormous bank vault, brightly illuminated by golden ceiling lights. Tall steel shelves packed with neatly stacked gold bars flank a spacious central aisle. A closed briefcase sits on a waist-high central pedestal. An already-open circular vault doorway is visible at the far end.

The original sentry patrols beside the pedestal, initially looking away from the hero's approach. Five reinforcements crouch out of sight behind low gold stacks at the shelf bases.

The hero descends behind the sentry's sightline, lands, disconnects from the rope, and approaches the briefcase. The rope stays behind him near the landing point.

Use shared frames to show the 188 cm hero towering over the 80 cm gremlins. The hero's right hand handles the revolver and later flicks the cap; his left hand carries the briefcase.

The gremlins' extreme sound sensitivity is a fictional creature trait. Their oversized ears reveal both their detection ability and their eventual weakness.

SECOND-BY-SECOND ACTION AND CAMERA

00–01:
Extreme macro on the original sentry's gigantic furry ear. It sharply TWITCHES at the very first instant. A rapid, precise push-in catches the flexing cartilage and individual hairs.

01–02:
Pull back in a low lateral arc to reveal the small sentry in the huge golden vault. Its long tail sways as it keeps watch beside the central pedestal.

02–03:
Crane and tilt toward an open ceiling vent behind the sentry. BLADE SHERIFF silently descends on a taut rope into the aisle.

03–04:
Track downward beside his descending body. His long leather coat hangs with realistic weight; the sentry remains turned away in the background.

04–05:
Push close to his sunglasses. Bright gold shelves and ceiling lights glide across the lenses. He remains intensely focused.

05–06:
Tilt down with him as both boots gently meet the floor. He disconnects from the rope and takes a silent, careful step toward the pedestal.

06–07:
Glide beside his extended right hand as it approaches the briefcase handle without touching it. A subjective heartbeat grows louder and faster.

07–08:
Rise into an extreme close-up of his brow above the sunglasses. One bead of sweat gathers, stretches, and detaches. The heartbeat intensifies.

08–09:
Track the single droplet down to the polished floor. It strikes with one unnaturally distinct, close-miked TIK. The heartbeat cuts off immediately.

09–10:
Whip-pan to the original sentry's ears. Both ears pivot precisely toward BLADE SHERIFF. The camera creeps around toward its newly alert eye.

10–11:
Continue the creeping arc as the sentry slowly turns toward him. Its alert expression becomes a mocking grin, exposing its uneven fangs.

11–12:
Slide laterally across the shelving gaps as FIVE reinforcements rise from crouches behind low gold stacks. Heads, caps, and huge ears emerge in an ominous coordinated reveal.

12–13:
Lift into a clear overhead view: exactly SIX gremlins form a ring around the single hero, with the pedestal just beyond his reach. Establish every attacker's position.

13–14:
Drop to gremlin height and sweep along the ring as all six bare their fangs and ROAR together. The hero stands at the center, dwarfing them.

14–15:
Pull rapidly into a wide, low view as all six crouch, extend their claws, and launch toward the hero simultaneously from their established positions.

15–16:
Enter brief slow motion. Orbit partway around the hero as all six attackers converge through the same physical space, claws leading, ears spread, tails trailing.

16–17:
Track tightly around his right side as he sweeps his coat backward and draws the revolver. The airborne gremlins remain visible beyond the moving coat; the draw is clear and continuous.

17–18:
Tilt sharply along his arm as he points the revolver STRAIGHT UP at the reinforced ceiling. The attackers close in. A tiny moment of near-silence precedes the shot.

18–19:
He fires EXACTLY ONE SHOT upward. Show one muzzle flash and one recoil, with a small distant ceiling impact. Snap to a wide view as a transparent, refractive pressure front expands from the gun through the golden room, bending reflections and lifting fine dust. Return to real-time motion with the report.

19–20:
Punch in on the original sentry as the sound reaches it. Its huge ears fold backward and ripple; its smug expression instantly turns to stunned panic. It grabs both ears and shrieks.

20–21:
Track across the other airborne attackers as they also cover their ears and lose coordination. Their claws stop reaching for the hero. The same single gunshot reverberates through the vault.

21–22:
Low lateral tracking follows the first three gremlins tumbling and sliding onto the floor. Gold bars rattle on their shelves; a few shift and clink, without a shelving collapse.

22–23:
Continue the motion into a wider view as the remaining three land and collapse in a staggered sequence. All six are now down, alive and dazed, clutching their ears. The hero remains upright.

23–24:
Push toward the hero as he calmly lowers the revolver and returns it to the same right-side holster. The original sentry still has its police cap.

24–25:
Track his LEFT hand lifting the closed briefcase cleanly by its handle from the pedestal. The last heavy echoes fade into small metallic rattles.

25–26:
Dolly backward in front of him as he strolls toward the already-open vault doorway, carrying the briefcase in his left hand. His coat swings with each measured step.

26–27:
Move to a low side angle. Beside his path, the original sentry weakly props up its upper body while remaining down on the floor, cap crooked. The hero briefly bends at the waist and knee, bringing his free right hand within natural reach.

27–28:
Tight tracking close-up of ONE casual right-finger flick against the cap brim. The cap pops off and spins away. He immediately straightens and continues walking.

28–29:
Follow the spinning cap down to the floor. It lands, the metal badge gives one tiny CLINK, and the cap settles beside the defeated sentry.

29–30:
Glide into a final close-up of the sentry's exposed ear, which reflexively twitches at that tiny clink. Rack focus to BLADE SHERIFF walking through the open vault doorway in the background, briefcase in hand. End on his unhurried departure and the sentry's humiliated reaction.

SOUND

No BGM, no rhythmic music, no musical stingers, no dialogue, no narration.

Use only environmental and action sound effects:
Faint ventilation, restrained leather movement, the rising subjective heartbeat, the single exaggerated sweat-drop impact, claws scraping metal, six creature roars, coat swish, revolver draw, ONE deep concussive gunshot, layered reflections of that SAME shot, nonverbal creature shrieks, falling bodies, rattling gold, the briefcase handle, measured boot steps, and the final cap-badge clink.

The heartbeat is a subjective tension effect, not a noise the guard hears. The sweat-drop impact is the detection trigger. Make the gunshot feel powerful through contrast and reverberation, without clipping or distortion.

CONSTRAINTS

Preserve the specified action order and the ear-weakness payoff. Slow motion is limited to the converging leap and the draw; keep the rest purposeful and moving.

The one bullet goes upward into the ceiling. The six gremlins are overwhelmed by the fictional sonic effect, not struck by bullets.

No blood, wounds, dismemberment, deaths, extra shots, lasers, magic beams, glowing energy rings, lightning, fireballs, room collapse, or additional combat moves.

No extra people or gremlins, changing scale, swapped costumes, fused bodies, duplicate heroes, floating weapons, teleporting props, or changing briefcases.

Keep the original sentry's cap on until the final finger flick.

No animation, illustration, comic rendering, subtitles, captions, title cards, added logos, or character-sheet text. Preserve the costume emblems visible in the reference.
