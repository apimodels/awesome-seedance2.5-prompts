````text
Seedance 2.5 Prompt
30 seconds, 16:9, photoreal live-action cinema. A broad Manhattan avenue at noon, clear blue sky, hard sunlight, glass skyscrapers. Frame people, vehicles and windows beside the creatures for scale.

Reference image 1 is the only hunter, a 2.5 m giant. Deep green armor with white stripes, a mask with glowing red eye slits, long black hair, fur and beast-skull shoulder armor, fang necklace, skull belt buckle, chains around his forearms. His dragon-headed, double-bladed battle-axe rides across his back until the bus is safe.
Reference image 2 defines every dragon. Each measures 30 m from snout to tail, with a wingspan of about 50 m. Match the crimson and white scales, black spines and pale wings patterned in red. Follow one lead dragon and one yellow school bus throughout.

Action movie camera work continues every 1 second. From 16 to 22 seconds, stay in one moving curbside wide shot for the swing, release and catch. Keep the dragon moving left to right in this shot.

00-01s A street-level wide opens on dozens of dragons already crossing between towers. A wing shadow sweeps across the moving yellow school bus.
01-02s Skim the curb past fleeing pedestrians as the downdraft rolls a yellow taxi onto its side.
02-03s Race alongside the bus. One dragon peels out of the swarm and dives toward the roof.
03-04s Track at roof height. Talons on both hind feet grip the roof edges. Metal creaks above the windows.
04-05s Dip to the tires as they lift clear of the street. The shadow of the bus slides away below.
05-06s Rise along the windows past frightened children. A child in a red sleeve presses a palm to the glass.
06-07s One-second cut-in. Push in on a mother on the sidewalk, hand over her mouth, eyes following the rising bus.
07-08s Cut wide. The hunter drops into the intersection and lands hard in the crosswalk.
08-09s Skim cracks racing out from his boots, then tilt up with his swinging forearm chains.
09-10s Push into his mask. Black hair whips past as the red eye slits flare.
10-11s Pull wide as he grips a traffic-light pole. Its yellow three-lens housing is visible above him.
11-12s Rise with the entire pole as he tears it out at the base, signal head still attached.
12-13s One-second cut-in. Slide past a food cart. The vendor freezes with his tongs raised. Beside him, a wide-eyed businessman stands motionless.
13-14s Track the lead dragon racing low between the towers, the same bus still in its claws.
14-15s Draw alongside. The bus hangs a few metres above the street. The dragon's head reaches low, ahead and to one side of the bus.
15-16s Widen to include the waiting hunter. The dragon flares its wings to brake in the broad intersection.
16-17s From the curb, pan as the hunter winds up. Frame the signal head, the dragon's head and the bus in profile. The horns fade to one chain clink.
17-18s Follow one full swing. The pole strikes the dragon's armored head with a metallic crack, knocking it down and away from the bus. Brief slow motion at contact.
18-19s Resume full speed. Track the recoiling dragon as both hind feet open. A gap appears between the talons and the roof.
19-20s Tilt down with the falling bus. The hunter flings the pole aside and opens both empty arms beneath it.
20-21s Ease wider as he catches the underside on both forearms. The bus stays level, every wheel clear of the road.
21-22s Sink with his knees. Asphalt caves in beneath his boots while he holds the bus above the crater.
22-23s Glide past a hanging tire. A piece of broken asphalt falls beside it. The tire stays still.
23-24s One-second cut-in. Two young people filming lower their phones just enough to look over them and cheer.
24-25s Track down as the hunter eases the bus toward the pavement, supporting it with both arms.
25-26s At wheel height, the tires touch down gently on either side of the dent. The suspension gives a soft squeak.
26-27s Inside the same window, the children look up at his mask. The red-sleeved child lifts that palm from the glass in a small wave.
27-28s Arc behind the hunter as he turns from the bus toward the sky and reaches over his shoulder.
28-29s Tilt up with the dragon-headed, double-bladed battle-axe as he draws it from his back.
29-30s Widen low over his shoulder. He raises the axe toward the swarm. Red wings bank toward him between the towers. Cut on their roar.

Sound effects only. Wingbeats, roars, car horns, screams, straining metal, chains, cracking asphalt, bus suspension and cheers. No BGM, dialogue, narration, subtitles, titles, logos, reference-sheet graphics, blood or gore.
````
